About Me

Academic and Professional Journey

I am currently pursuing a Master's degree in Computer Science at the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign (UIUC), having previously earned my Bachelor's in Mathematics-Computer Science from UC San Diego.

My technical focus lies at the intersection of machine learning and autonomous systems, with a particular interest in navigation, reinforcement learning, and state abstraction. I enjoy tackling complex, system-level challenges, drawing on my backend engineering experience at Vipshop and OfSpectrum, where I designed scalable e-commerce features and built robust event notification systems from the ground up.

Whether I am developing full-stack web platforms, exploring C++ game development, or serving as a teaching assistant for advanced data structures, I am driven by a passion for building accessible, data-driven solutions that bridge the gap between theoretical algorithms and real-world applications.
