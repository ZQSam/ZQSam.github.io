01 / THE CHALLENGE

A signal is only
the beginning.

A trade alert is usually a short, informal message. Turning it into something a system can evaluate means resolving contract details, interpreting context, applying risk constraints, and keeping a record of what happened.

I built Financial Agent to connect these steps in one observable system, with paper trading and performance review built into the workflow.

AI with a review loop

A language-model parser extracts structured option data. A vision model interprets chart-image alerts. Ambiguous parses can be reviewed and corrected in the dashboard, with corrections captured for later analysis.

Risk policies before execution

The risk engine supports per-trade spend limits, stop-loss and trim settings, daily loss limits, ticker restrictions, and trader eligibility gates. Account-specific policies allow the same signal to be evaluated under different constraints.

Paper trading as a comparison tool

Signals are evaluated across current, suggested, and baseline paper strategies. Fill models and shadow orders help compare assumptions with observed executions. Live brokerage execution is a separate, gated capability.

02 / THE ENGINEERING

Connected services.
A shared picture.

A Next.js dashboard presents the agent’s activity. A FastAPI backend handles the financial workflow, while an Express gateway relays status updates. PostgreSQL stores durable records, and Redis supports live state and messaging.

03 / OPERATING THE SYSTEM

Make every step
inspectable.

A dashboard for the whole workflow

Signal review, account summaries, strategy comparisons, analytics, and subscription-cost tracking bring the moving parts together.

Health checks and reporting

Pre-market checks inspect the pipeline, record health reports, and surface issues. Quote-coverage checks and performance reporting make the system easier to monitor and maintain.
